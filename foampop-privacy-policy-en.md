# Privacy Policy – **ASMR Foam Pop**

**Effective Date:** 17 August 2025  
Contact: rretamal.dev@live.com

---

## Introduction

ASMR Foam Pop (“the **App**”) is a casual bubble-popping game.  
We do **not** ask you to create an account, and we collect only the data strictly needed to run and improve the game and to show ads.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Device & Usage Data

| Data                           | Example                                      | Purpose                                  |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Advertising ID** (AAID/IDFA) | `a1b2c3...`                                  | Ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention |
| **Device info**                | OS version, model                            | Debug, analytics                         |
| **App info**                   | App version, session length, screens visited | Improve features, fix crashes            |

Processed via **Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics** (Google).

### 1.2 Audio & Haptic

- The App **plays local sound files** and triggers **haptic feedback**.
- **We do not access or record** your microphone, camera, or precise location.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- Deliver core gameplay and haptic/audio feedback
- Analyse performance (crash reports, aggregated analytics)
- Show advertising via **Google AdMob**
- Combat fraud and maintain security
- Respond to support requests

---

## 3. Ads & Consent (EEA/UK/CH)

We use **Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP)**, a Google-certified CMP:

- **If you grant consent** → personalised ads may be shown.
- **If you refuse** → only **Non-Personalised Ads (NPA)** are served.
- You can reopen the consent form at any time in **Settings ▸ Privacy**.

---

## 4. Information Sharing

We never sell your data.  
We share it only with:

| Provider                              | Purpose                  | Policy                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Google AdMob**                      | Ads & measurement        | <https://policies.google.com/privacy>         |
| **Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics)** | Usage stats & crash logs | <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy> |

All providers operate under contracts that require them to keep data secure and confidential.

---

## 5. Data Security

- Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Controlled access to servers (Google Cloud)
- Regular security reviews and updates

---

## 6. Data Retention

| Data Type        | Retention Period                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Analytics events | 14 months                                   |
| Crash logs       | 90 days                                     |
| Advertising IDs  | Reset when you reinstall or reset IDFA/AAID |

---

## 7. Your Rights

- **GDPR (EEA/UK):** access, rectification, deletion, restrict processing, data portability, withdraw consent.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California):** right to know, delete, opt-out of sale/share (we do not sell).

Email **rretamal.dev@live.com** with your country & request; we reply within 30 days.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The App targets users **13 years and older** and is **not directed to children under 13** (COPPA). If you believe we collected data from a child, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## 9. Third-Party Links

The App does not contain external links or social-media sharing. Future versions will place any external link behind a **parental gate**.
